PCS FIELD REFERENCE

Field guide

The essential addresses and operating notes, available without Internet access.

Get connected

  1. Join the PCS access point or connect to its LAN.
  2. Open http://10.42.0.1/ in your browser.
  3. Check the health indicator, then choose a service.

pcs.local may also work when local hostname discovery is configured. The IP address remains the dependable fallback.

Understand PCS health

● OK — The existing PCS checks report healthy operation.

▲ WARN — A component needs attention or has degraded operation.

✕ BAD — PCS reports a fault that needs an operator’s attention.

OK - OFFLINE — PCS is healthy without an Internet uplink. Local services can still work. Other warnings or faults remain visible.

Status unavailable means current readings cannot be obtained; it is not a new PCS health state. Open Status or contact an administrator.

Radio services

APRS provides packet-radio station and messaging information. Meshtastic connects the configured mesh radio to the PCS gateway. Both are optional; “Not configured” is expected on appliances without them.

View radio services

Administration and help

The public portal is read-only. Authorized operators use Admin for service controls, backups, networking and shutdown. Cockpit is a separate system-management login.

Forgetting a PCS admin password requires recovery from a local Pi terminal. Ask the responsible administrator.

PCS FIELD REFERENCE

Field guide

Quick-start, service and degraded-operation guidance, available without Internet access.

Start PCS

  1. Connect either the AC inlet or a suitable DC source. Never connect independent power sources at the same time.
  2. Make sure the PCS storage drive is connected, then select the intended source and switch on AC at the inlet when using AC.
  3. Allow the display and indicators to complete startup. PCS may still become usable while GPS, an uplink or an optional service is unavailable.
  4. Check Overview or Status for warnings before beginning field operations.

Use the Power page to confirm the selected source and live rail measurements. Battery mode protects a nominal 12V source with a low-voltage alarm and controlled-shutdown countdown; Power Supply mode disables that battery cutoff.

Connect a device

  1. Join the Portable Comm Server access point or connect by Ethernet to the PCS LAN.
  2. Open http://10.42.0.1/ in your browser.
  3. Check the health indicator, then choose a service.

pcs.local may also work when local hostname discovery is configured. The IP address remains the dependable fallback.

Power and shutdown

The source selector keeps the internal AC supply and external DC input separate. Do not combine them, and do not treat the selector as a battery disconnect.

On a nominal 12V battery, PCS confirms low voltage before starting its 90-second controlled shutdown. Recovery clears the countdown. After PCS has shut down, disconnect the battery because passive hardware can continue to draw power.

Energy and remaining-capacity values are estimates. They do not replace the voltage protection or an external battery-management system.

Understand PCS health

● OK — The existing PCS checks report healthy operation.

▲ WARN — A component needs attention or has degraded operation.

✕ BAD — PCS reports a fault that needs an operator’s attention.

OK - OFFLINE — PCS is healthy without an Internet uplink. Local services can still work. Other warnings or faults remain visible.

Status unavailable means current readings cannot be obtained; it is not a new PCS health state. Open Status or contact an administrator.

Files and time

Connect to \\10.42.0.1\PCS-Share for primary shared storage. Connect to \\10.42.0.1\PCS-Backup using the PCS backup account and administrator password.

Keep storage connected until an administrator has safely unmounted it. Check storage health on Status if a share is unavailable.

PCS serves NTP to LAN clients at 10.42.0.1. It prefers GPS time, can use Internet time when available, and retains RTC holdover for offline starts.

Radio services

APRS uses the installed packet-radio station for beacons, digipeating and messaging. Current station, frequency, modem and APRS-IS health are shown on Radio services. Send HELP or H to the configured PCS APRS address for the supported status commands.

Meshtastic uses the attached RAK4631 as a USB-connected mesh/MQTT gateway and can receive its position from PCS GPSD. Current radio, MQTT and position-feed health are shown on Radio services.

Pi-Star and Starlink

Pi-Star is normally available at 10.42.0.3 when paired. PCS can provide its LAN time and GPSD feed and can request a coordinated shutdown.

Starlink Mini can serve as the preferred Ethernet WAN when commissioned and healthy. Connect its Ethernet path before relying on it, then use the Starlink tab for dish telemetry and the Power tab for passthrough-branch measurements.

Degraded and offline operation

The PCS LAN, homepage, file shares, radio services and local time distribution do not require an Internet uplink. A missing uplink is a degraded condition, not a loss of the local appliance.

Without GPS, PCS can fall back to Internet time or its RTC. Without Internet, APRS RF and local Meshtastic traffic can continue, while APRS-IS, MQTT, Pi-Star Internet modes, WireGuard and other remote services may be unavailable.

Check Status to distinguish an expected offline condition from a hardware or service fault. Authorized operators can use Admin to mute warning alerts without hiding their visual status.

Administration

The public portal is read-only. Authorized operators use Admin for service controls, backups, networking, alert mute and shutdown. A forgotten PCS admin password requires recovery from a local Pi terminal.
